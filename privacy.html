Privacy

Effective 2 May 2026

This explains what data the carparks.je iOS app collects, why, where it goes, and what you can do about it. Plain English. No dark patterns.

We're a small Jersey-based operation. The data controller for everything below is Parking Intelligence Limited, trading as carparks.je. Contact: privacy@carparks.je.

The two-pipe model

The app keeps two strictly separate kinds of data. The distinction matters — please read both.

On-device only

Your precise location, full search queries, the destinations you actually pick, the parks you focus on, your trip starts and arrivals — these are recorded into an encrypted SwiftData store inside the app sandbox on your iPhone. They never leave your device. They power features like personalised park suggestions ("you usually pick Green Street on Wednesdays around 09:00"). You can wipe this store at any time from Settings → Privacy → Clear on-device history. We can't see it. Apple can't see it. iCloud doesn't sync it.

Server-side analytics

When the app does something useful (you start a trip, search for a destination, view a recommendation), it sends a small de-identified event to our servers in BigQuery. These events deliberately do not include your precise coordinates or what you searched for. They include coarse area buckets (e.g. ST_HELIER, OUTSIDE_KNOWN_AREA), category labels (e.g. healthcare, shop), and distance bands (e.g. 1_3km). Each event carries an opaque per-device identifier (Apple's IDFV) and a per-launch session id. We use this to spot bugs, prioritise features, and improve our parking recommendations.

You can turn the server pipe off entirely from Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics. Doing so does not affect any app functionality.

What's in a server-side event

Each event includes:

  • a generated event id and timestamp
  • the device's IDFV — an Apple-assigned identifier specific to our app, which changes if you uninstall and reinstall
  • a per-launch session id
  • the event name (e.g. start_trip, destination_selected)
  • the app version, OS version, device model, locale, timezone
  • coarse parameters relevant to that event — area buckets, category buckets, distance bands, park codes, durations

We do not collect:

  • your name, email, phone number, or contacts
  • precise GPS coordinates
  • the literal text you type into the search bar
  • the photos, contacts, or other apps on your device

Legal basis

We rely on legitimate interest (Jersey Data Protection Law 2018 art. 8(1)(f) / UK GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) for the server-side analytics pipe — specifically, our interest in operating, improving, and debugging a free public-good app.

We've completed a Legitimate Interest Assessment, which concludes that this basis is valid because (a) precise coordinates and search-text content are bucketed on your device before anything is transmitted — there is no precise version of this data on our servers, ever; (b) raw event access is restricted to the data controller via Google Cloud IAM; (c) the user can opt out at any time without losing functionality; (d) no profiling or automated decision-making affects the user.

The on-device pipe is not subject to data protection law because the data never leaves the user's device. We are not the controller of data we cannot access.

Retention

  • Server-side events: 18 months (BigQuery partition expiration). After that, they're automatically deleted.
  • On-device events: kept until you delete them. We've deliberately chosen not to auto-prune so the personalised-suggestion features have a long signal history. Use Settings → Privacy → Clear on-device history to wipe.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. We do use Google Cloud (BigQuery, Cloud Functions) and Firebase (Analytics, App Check, Cloud Messaging) as data processors. Their privacy policies apply to the data we hand them. We don't share with anyone else.

Your rights

Under Jersey's Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018 and (where applicable) the UK GDPR, you can:

  • ask us what we hold about your device (subject access request)
  • ask us to delete it (right to erasure)
  • ask us to correct it (right to rectification — though our event data is largely immutable, so this rarely applies)
  • object to processing (which is how the in-app analytics toggle works)
  • complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@carparks.je. We aim to respond within 30 days. To make a deletion request actionable, please send the request from the device itself using Settings → Privacy → Request server-side deletion — this gives us the IDFV needed to locate your records.

Children

The app is rated 4+ on the App Store but is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we'll delete what we can.

Changes

We'll bump the effective date at the top and post the diff in the app's release notes when we change this policy materially.
