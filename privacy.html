Privacy

Effective 2 July 2026

carparks.je is a free app with no accounts. We don't know who you are, and we'd like to keep it that way. This page lists everything the app processes, in plain English.

What stays on your phone

Your precise location (used for the map, walking routes, and drive times), the text you type into search, and your history of places and trips (which powers smart suggestions) are stored only on your device. None of it is sent to our servers. You can wipe the history any time in Settings → Privacy → Clear on-device history. Location is "while using the app" only; we never track you in the background. Destination search itself is provided by Apple Maps, so your searches are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What reaches our servers

1. Usage analytics — optional

The app sends small events like "trip started" or "recommendation viewed" so we can spot bugs and improve the recommendations. Before anything is sent, locations are reduced on your device to coarse buckets — a parish or town zone, a distance band, a place category like shop — so precise coordinates and search text never reach us in any form. Each event carries Apple's per-app device identifier (IDFV, which resets if you reinstall), a per-launch session id, and basics like app version, OS version, device model, locale and timezone. Events are kept for 18 months, then deleted automatically.

You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics. The switch disables both our own events and Google Analytics collection, and nothing in the app stops working.

2. Trips — only while one is running

Starting a trip (the Live Activity on your lock screen) stores the trip on our servers so we can keep it updated: the destination you chose (name and map position), arrival time, the recommended car park, a random identifier generated on your device, and an Apple push token used to update the Live Activity. This is the feature working as advertised, not analytics — and it's deleted when the trip ends (8 hours at most). Your own location is not part of it.

3. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a report with the stack trace, device model and OS version so we can fix it. No location, no identity.

What we never collect

  • your name, email, phone number, or contacts — there are no accounts
  • precise GPS coordinates or search text
  • advertising identifiers — there are no ads, and we never sell or share data

Who processes it

Our servers run on Google Cloud and Firebase (Google), and push notifications and map search go through Apple. Both act as processors for the data described above; we share with no one else.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018 you can ask what we hold, ask us to delete it, or object to processing (that's what the analytics switch does). For deletion, use Settings → Privacy → Request server-side deletion — sending from the device gives us the identifier needed to find your records. You can also complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes materially, we'll bump the date above and note it in the app's release notes. Questions and requests: privacy@carparks.je.
